ONE BREAKER — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 10 October 2026

ONE BREAKER is a single-player Android game by Z Lab for a general audience. It is not designed for children. You can play offline. Advertising test builds use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices. Test builds use Google's demo ads; a future public advertising build may use revenue-generating ads. The game has no player accounts, purchases, Z Lab server or separate analytics service.

Local saves

The game stores unlocked and completed levels, audio volume, vibration preferences and the version of the Terms of Use you accepted in its private app storage. These values restore your progress and settings. Temporary or backup files may exist while saving. The game does not send these save files to Z Lab or Google.

Device features

Vibration provides optional feedback and can be disabled in Settings. The game does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or shared files.

Advertising and privacy choices

When advertising services connect, Google may process your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. An IP address may indicate a general location. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing.

Advertising builds request non-personalized ads with a G content rating, disable publisher first-party IDs and remove Android advertising-ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions. The owner-configured consent test is set as a general audience app. Earlier demo builds used child-directed treatment for testing. These settings do not eliminate all processing by Google.

In builds that use UMP, Google's configured privacy messages collect choices where required. Accepting the game Terms is separate from advertising consent. A Privacy Options button appears in Settings when Google reports that it is required. Ad requests follow the SDK's permission state; ordinary gameplay remains available when ads are unavailable.

Automatic ads are limited to leaving won results, with a ten-minute interval after a displayed full-screen ad closes. After a loss, a player can choose a rewarded recovery once per attempt. The Terms explain the timing and reward rules.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data information for information about Google's services.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. This removes progress and preferences. Device backup and restore behaviour depends on Android and your account settings. Google manages data processed through its services under its own policies; clearing local game storage does not delete Google-held data.

Store services

The store used to download ONE BREAKER operates its own service under its privacy policy. This game does not add a TapTap login or send local game saves to TapTap.
